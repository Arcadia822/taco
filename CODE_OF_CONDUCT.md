# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Project discussions should remain technical, specific, and useful. Participants are expected to:

- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Provide enough evidence for others to reproduce claims.
- Respect differing experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Keep feedback focused on the project and its documented scope.
- Stop disruptive behavior when a maintainer asks.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its participants. Enforcement decisions should be based on observed conduct and applied consistently.

Use GitHub's reporting and moderation tools to report conduct concerns privately when available. Security vulnerabilities must follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not this process.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces represented as part of the Taco project.
